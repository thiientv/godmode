# Architecture candidate report

For each candidate include:

```text
Candidate:
Observed friction and evidence:
Current owner/interface and dependency direction:
Proposed owner/interface and dependency direction:
Why this increases locality or leverage:
Testability change:
Migration and compatibility risk:
Existing ADR or domain-language impact:
Smallest useful first move:
Non-goals:
Confidence: strong | worth exploring | speculative
```

End with a prioritized recommendation and explain why doing nothing is or is
not acceptable. Do not equate more layers with better architecture.
